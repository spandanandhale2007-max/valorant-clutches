# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Made from the game-events template of the Clips Kitty SDK 1.3.0.
- Finds Valorant fights from bursts of change in the kill feed's corner of the screen, labelled `multikill`, `ace` or `clutch`.
- Names a fight an ace or a clutch from the transcript (`words_ace`, `words_clutch`), and scores it higher when the sound gets louder.
